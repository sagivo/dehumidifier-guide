Three brands own the serious whole-house dehumidifier market, and they don't compete on the same terms. AprilAire sells through HVAC pros with honest ratings and top efficiency. AlorAir sells direct with unbeatable pints-per-dollar and marketing numbers you have to decode. Santa Fe sells build quality and filtration with the longest warranties. Here's how to pick your camp.

## Brand philosophies at a glance

| | AprilAire | AlorAir | Santa Fe |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rating style | AHAM, honest | Saturation marketing (decode to AHAM) | AHAM, honest |
| Price band | ~$1,550–2,600 | ~$506–1,147 | ~$1,300–3,200 |
| Efficiency | Energy Star Most Efficient (E080/E100/E130) | Energy Star (HD55, HD90) | Strong, less badge-heavy |
| Warranty | 5 years | 5–6 years | 6 years |
| Origin | Made in USA | Value import | US-built reputation |
| Channel | HVAC pro / contractor | Direct / online | Direct + dealer |
| Standout trait | Efficiency + honest ratings | Pints per dollar, built-in pumps | Filtration + build quality |

## AprilAire: the professional's choice

AprilAire is the brand your HVAC contractor already installs. The E-series — E070 (70 pt, ~$1,550–1,770), E080 (80 pt, ~$1,750–1,900), E100 Pro (100 pt, ~$2,015–2,300), E130 (130 pt, ~$2,495–2,600) — is rated in honest AHAM pints, carries Energy Star Most Efficient on the three larger models, and is built in the USA with a 5-year warranty.

What you're really buying: **certainty**. The ratings are real, the efficiency is independently certified, and when something needs service in year four, there's a contractor network that knows the unit. The cost is literal — AprilAire charges roughly 2–3x AlorAir's prices for comparable AHAM capacity. For a ducted whole-home install where a pro is doing the work anyway, that premium buys peace of mind that's hard to argue against. Our [best-overall pick](/posts/best-whole-house-dehumidifiers-2026/) is the E100 Pro for exactly these reasons.

**Buy AprilAire if:** you're doing a ducted install with a contractor, you want the best efficiency ratings, or you value honest specs and serviceability over upfront savings.

## AlorAir: the value disruptor

AlorAir's proposition is simple: far more AHAM pints per dollar than anyone else. The Sentinel HD55 delivers 55 AHAM pints for ~$506–638. The HD90 delivers 90 AHAM pints (Energy Star) for ~$1,000–1,100. The HDi90 adds a **built-in condensate pump** — the killer feature for crawl spaces — at ~$1,032–1,147. Warranties run 5–6 years.

The catch is the marketing: those "120-pint" and "198-pint" headlines are saturation numbers, roughly 2x the AHAM figures. Once you decode them (see [our explainer](/posts/aham-vs-saturation-pints-explained/)), the value story is genuinely strong — just not the miracle the box suggests. Build quality is value-tier: perfectly adequate for a basement or crawl space workhorse, but don't expect Santa Fe fit and finish.

AlorAir's other edge is the **pump lineup**. The HDi90's built-in pump solves the crawl-space drainage problem at the unit, which neither AprilAire nor Santa Fe matches at that price. If your install location has no gravity drain, AlorAir deserves a serious look.

**Buy AlorAir if:** budget matters most, the job is a basement or crawl space rather than a showpiece install, or you need a built-in pump without paying premium prices.

## Santa Fe: the build-quality play

Santa Fe sits between the other two on price and above both on certain quality dimensions. The Compact70 (70 AHAM, ~$1,300–1,450) is 12 inches tall — purpose-built for crawl spaces with tight clearance — and the Advance90 (90 AHAM, ~$1,800–3,200) adds dual exhausts for flexible installs. Both carry **MERV-13 filtration**, a genuine differentiator if the dehumidifier shares air-quality duty, and both carry a **6-year warranty**, the longest standard coverage in this comparison.

Santa Fe's pricing quirk: the Advance90's street price swings widely (~$1,800–3,200) by channel, so shop it carefully. You're paying for American-built reputation, filtration, and warranty length — not for maximum pints per dollar (AlorAir wins that) or maximum efficiency badges (AprilAire wins that).

**Buy Santa Fe if:** indoor air quality matters (MERV-13 built in), your crawl space clearance is tight (Compact70), or you want the longest warranty in the class.

## Head-to-head by use case

- **Ducted whole-home install, pro doing the work:** AprilAire E100 Pro or E080. The contractor channel and efficiency badges justify the premium.
- **Crawl space, value priority:** AlorAir HDi90 — 90 AHAM pints with a built-in pump at ~$1,032–1,147 is unmatched value for the job. Full planning in the [crawl space guide](/posts/crawl-space-dehumidifier-guide/).
- **Crawl space, tight clearance or premium build:** Santa Fe Compact70.
- **Small home or single damp zone, tight budget:** AlorAir HD55 — 55 AHAM pints under ~$650.
- **Air quality + dehumidification in one:** Santa Fe Advance90 with MERV-13.
- **Big home, heavy moisture load:** AprilAire E130 — 130 AHAM pints, nothing else in this lineup touches it.

## The honest summary

There's no overall winner — there's a winner **for your job**. AprilAire wins on trust and efficiency, AlorAir wins on value and pump options, Santa Fe wins on build and filtration. Compare them in AHAM pints, match the brand's strength to your installation reality, and you'll end up with the right unit rather than the most heavily marketed one.

## A note on service and parts

One underrated brand difference: **serviceability over a decade**. AprilAire's contractor network means filters, parts, and technicians are easy to find in year seven — a real advantage for a ducted unit buried in an attic. Santa Fe's 6-year warranty is the longest promise in the group, and their US-based support has a strong reputation among crawl-space contractors. AlorAir's direct/online model keeps prices low but means you're more likely to be troubleshooting via support tickets than calling a local tech who stocks the parts. For a basement or crawl-space unit you can walk up to, that trade is usually fine; for a ducted install inside finished walls, weigh it carefully.
