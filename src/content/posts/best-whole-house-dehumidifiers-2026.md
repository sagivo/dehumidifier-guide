Shopping for a whole-house dehumidifier is confusing for one big reason: the industry doesn't label capacity the same way twice. AprilAire and Santa Fe publish **AHAM ratings** — pints per day measured at the standardized 80°F / 60% RH test condition — while AlorAir advertises **saturation numbers** measured near 90°F / 90% RH, which run roughly 2x the AHAM figure. The AlorAir "120-pint" HD55 is really a 55-pint AHAM unit. Same machine, different honesty.

This ranking is built on **AHAM pints only**, so every unit below is compared fairly. We don't lab-test units; these rankings are research-based — manufacturer specs, Energy Star certification data, installer community feedback, and warranty terms. Check current prices before buying; street prices move around.

## 1. AprilAire E100 Pro — Best overall

The E100 Pro is the unit we'd recommend to the most homeowners. 100 honest AHAM pints cover up to ~5,500 sq ft — enough for a large home with a damp basement — and it carries **Energy Star Most Efficient** certification (shared with the E080 and E130), so it removes more moisture per kilowatt-hour than the typical competition. The 5-year warranty and Made-in-USA build come with the deep contractor network AprilAire enjoys: if you want a ducted install, your HVAC company almost certainly already installs these.

~$2,015–2,300 is premium money, but you're paying for honest ratings, efficiency, and serviceability — not marketing. For most whole-home applications, this is the reference point everything else gets judged against.

## 2. AprilAire E130 — Most capacity

When the house is big or the moisture load is serious — a 5,000+ sq ft home on the Gulf Coast, a walkout basement with seepage history — the E130's 130 AHAM pints and 7,200 sq ft of coverage are the biggest hammer in this lineup. Same Energy Star Most Efficient badge, same 5-year warranty, same Made-in-USA construction. At ~$2,495–2,600 it costs only modestly more than the E100 Pro for 30% more capacity, which makes it the value move for genuinely large moisture loads. Oversized for a 2,000 sq ft colonial; perfect for the house where 100 pints would be running flat-out all summer.

## 3. Santa Fe Advance90 — Best premium alternative

Santa Fe plays in the same premium lane as AprilAire with a different philosophy: build quality and air quality. The Advance90 delivers 90 AHAM pints for up to 3,000 sq ft, adds **dual exhausts** for flexible installation, and ships with **MERV-13 filtration** — meaningful if your dehumidifier is sharing duty with the home's air quality. The **6-year warranty** is the longest standard coverage in this roundup. Pricing is the quirk: it swings from ~$1,800 to ~$3,200 depending on channel, so shop carefully. If you want filtration built into your dehumidifier and a warranty that outlasts the competition, this is your unit. Full head-to-head: [AprilAire vs AlorAir vs Santa Fe](/posts/aprilaire-vs-alorair-vs-santa-fe/).

## 4. AprilAire E080 — Sweet spot for mid-size homes

80 AHAM pints and 4,400 sq ft of coverage make the E080 the right size for a typical 2,500–3,500 sq ft home. It's Energy Star Most Efficient, carries the same 5-year warranty, and at ~$1,750–1,900 it sits in a reasonable middle between the budget tier and the 100+ pint flagships. If the E100 Pro is more than your home needs, this is usually the correct downsizing rather than jumping to a different brand.

## 5. AlorAir Sentinel HDi90 — Best for crawl spaces

Crawl spaces are a special job — low clearance, no nearby drain, often vented and nasty — and the HDi90 is purpose-built for it: 90 AHAM pints with a **built-in condensate pump**, so the drain problem is solved at the unit instead of in your plumbing plan. At ~$1,032–1,147 it's roughly half the price of the premium brands for comparable AHAM capacity. The trade-off is AlorAir's saturation marketing (the box says 198 pints — it's a 90 AHAM unit) and a value-tier build, but for a crawl space where the unit sits on a vapor barrier doing its job unseen, that's a rational trade. Warranty runs 5–6 years depending on terms. Pair it with our [crawl space dehumidifier guide](/posts/crawl-space-dehumidifier-guide/) for encapsulation and drainage planning.

## 6. Santa Fe Compact70 — Best low-clearance build

At just 12 inches tall, the Compact70 fits crawl spaces that swallow bigger units whole. It delivers 70 AHAM pints for up to 2,200 sq ft of crawl space, includes MERV-13 filtration, and carries a 6-year warranty — Santa Fe's build quality in a package that fits where others can't. ~$1,300–1,450 positions it between AlorAir value and AprilAire premium. If your crawl space clearance is tight and you want a unit that will live there for a decade-plus, this is the one.

## 7. AlorAir Sentinel HD55 — Best value

The HD55 is the gateway whole-house unit: 55 AHAM pints, up to 1,300 sq ft, at ~$506–638 — less than a third of the premium flagships. It's Energy Star certified and fine for smaller homes, single large basements, or targeted zones (that one damp wing of the house). It won't carry a whole 3,000 sq ft home in August, and you should ignore the 120-pint box number entirely. But as a value play for the right-sized job, nothing else touches it.

## How to pick from this list

- **Standard whole-home need (2,500–5,000 sq ft):** Start with the E100 Pro, downsize to the E080 if the math says so.
- **Big home or heavy moisture load:** E130.
- **Crawl space:** HDi90 (built-in pump, best price) or Compact70 (tight clearance, best build).
- **Air quality matters too:** Advance90 with MERV-13.
- **Tight budget, smaller job:** HD55.
